# News Research

Analyze news events and their impact on the AI supply chain watchlist.

## Steps

1. Check financial news sources for AI, semiconductor, and technology headlines.
2. For each significant news item, determine:
   - Which watchlist stocks are affected?
   - Is this temporary (sentiment/noise) or structural (thesis-changing)?
   - Has this happened before? Check memory for similar events.
3. Categorize the impact:
   - **Earnings:** Company reported results (beat/miss/guide)
   - **Geopolitical:** Taiwan tensions, China export controls, sanctions
   - **Competition:** New product launch, market share shift, customer win/loss
   - **Macro:** Interest rates, recession fears, currency moves
   - **Supply chain:** Shortage, capacity expansion, pricing changes
4. For thesis-changing news, flag for immediate alert.
5. For noise, note it but don't recommend action.

## Output

Brief Telegram alert for significant news:
- Headline summary
- Affected stocks from watchlist
- Impact assessment (temporary vs structural)
- Recommended action (if any)
- Memory reference (similar past events)
